## Non-negotiable identity — approximately 90% human
- **Clearly human female faces** (human-shaped nose, cheeks, lips and jaw, eyes/hair) and primarily human body anatomy; **do NOT use a full hyena snout, protruding muzzle, entirely furred face or elongated animal jaw**. Human face first; small pointed canine teeth can show in the smile.
- Prominent *rounded hyena ears* atop the head, mischievous **iconic toothy hyena grin**, spotted hyena markings on skin and the body, visible hyena tails (short to full/plumed/banded) and selective shaggy fur.
- A diverse, generally **darker-skinned palette**: caramel, bronze, umber, ebony with sandy/light and ash-violet variants as additional options. Fur/spot patterns are clearly visible on all tones. Skin tones and coat pattern choices are visual only, **never** tied to intrinsic intelligence, personality, job aptitude or racial stat bonuses.
- Rich fur concentrated around **outer thighs and hips**, extending along **forearms, wrists and backs of the hands like paw-gloves** (with occasional calves and ankle accents). Hands/fingers and feet still read **human-shaped** with expressive clawlike nails and optional soft paw details; not fully animal forelegs/feet.
- Alternating **classic spots, dense/clustered spots, rosettes, stripe mix, light/faded markings** across shoulders, upper arms, thighs, and lower limbs. Contrasting light mane-like fur can surround collar/shoulders without covering face.
- Feminine adult builds include **lithe/toned, athletic/balanced, powerful/muscular and plump/voluptuous**; strong thighs/hips and visible muscle where appropriate. Maintain a recognizable part-human anatomy at every body type.

## Canonical reference-sheet panels
- Six original illustrative profession looks: **Worker, Warrior, Ranger, Mage, Tinkerer, Elder**.
- Expressions: Neutral, Happy, Mischievous Grin, Laughing, Smug, Fierce, Surprised, Playful/Wink. **Smile is iconic, but not every expression must grin**.
- Rounded, tufted, notched, tassel/decorative hyena ears; standard bushy, fluffy, spotted-tip, banded hyena tails.
- Pattern/texture panels: spotted, rosette, savannah stripe mix, subtle; thigh fur, forearm fur, paw-glove, claws; tone and body-type palette panels.
- Front/three-quarter/side/back silhouettes should hold the **human face and body** but show integrated hyena ears, skin spots, selective fur and tail.
- Source shows approximate **1.60–1.90 m** adult female size as a **concept illustration only**, not a verified in-game value.
