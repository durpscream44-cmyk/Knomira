## Canonical anatomy and distinction
- Fully **human** adult female silhouettes, clearly human ears and faces, conventional hands and feet, no horns, tails, scales, muzzles or animal ears.
- Human-height proportions with a diversity of natural skin tones, hair color/texture and hairstyles, eye colors, and body builds. The concept sheet shows **~1.55–1.80 m adult female**, an *illustrative* measure not verified against game sprite or collider scale.
- The user requested **skin colors** as the primary visible variant axis. Do not tie complexion, geographical labels, hairstyles or appearance to personality, class, intelligence, biome adaptation, inherent racial abilities, or gameplay bonuses.
- Skin-tone swatches/portrait examples in the approved sheet span pale, fair, olive, tan, brown, deep, dark, and ebony. These are visual color names only; all roles and body types may use any skin tone.
- Render body options as **Athletic (lean/fit)**, **Curvy (balanced)**, **Voluptuous (fuller)** and **Strong (muscular)** as in the accepted image. Do not impose one silhouette on all humans.
