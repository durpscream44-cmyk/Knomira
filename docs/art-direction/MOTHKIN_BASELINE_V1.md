## Species silhouette, anatomy and morphology lock
- **Adult female Mothkin**, fully fantastical moth-insectoid; soft fur-like thorax/neck/shoulder ruffs and natural plate-like chitin, **lithe athletic but shapely/toned** body builds rather than the stocky Beetlekin silhouette or harder armored Antkin.
- **Large feathered/plumed antennae**, expressive gentle mothlike heads with **luminous purple/violet eyes**, moth-shaped cheek/neck fluff, delicate chitin accents and moth wings. Preserve mothlike anatomy in face profiles; do not turn them into ordinary humans with moth accessories.
- **Large patterned wings** held from the back, recognizable flutter/moth wing designs with dark borders, colored veining, spots/crescents/bands, optional faint magical bioluminescent effects. The wing arrangement must be coherent from multiple angles; no invented animation anchors or gameplay mechanics.
- Rich gold accents, cloth wraps/robes, layered fantasy leather and pouches, herbal/lantern accessories, and deep purple/violet vs pale fluffy contrast. The user approved this exact design rather than an alternate generated summary.
