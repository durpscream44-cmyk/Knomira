## Canonical anatomy
Adult female regular elves, graceful **slender elongated bipedal silhouette** compared with stout Rabbitkin/Tanukikin and curvier Dark Elfkin; slender waist, narrow arms/legs, elegant balanced bust and hip proportions, proportionally long legs, narrow shoulders, pointed elvish ears (standard longer elf proportions). Mature expressive anime-fantasy faces. The conceptual chart annotates about **1.6–1.8 m** tall; not a verified in-engine metric.

## Visual styling
Matches Knomira's `RABBITKIN_STYLE_LOCK.md` for ornate crunchy pixel clusters, dark contour lines, crisp stepped highlights, rich layered fantasy fabrics, delicate gold hardware, parchment/gold sheet presentation and legibility at native resolution. Regular Elfkin costumes include softer whites, lavender, crimson, forest green, dark plum, gold trim and floral accents without forcing those shades on every elf type.

## Approved visual role examples
Worker (gatherer/hauler), Warrior (sword/shield guard), Ranger (forest bow), Mage (violet crystal staff), Tinkerer (goggles/tools), Elder (book and scholar robes). These are costume examples, not implemented gameplay jobs.
