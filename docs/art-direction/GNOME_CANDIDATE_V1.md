## Candidate visual features
- Fully humanoid **adult female gnomes**: short (reference says ~0.90–1.20 m), stocky/compact, sturdy legs and hands, rounded expressive faces, *somewhat pointed gnome ears*, practical fantasy gear and bright individual hair coloring. These visual measurements are not verified in engine.
- Mature curvy, athletic, strong and fuller body silhouettes without inadvertently stretching them to human or elf height. Distinct from Rabbitkin, Tanukikin and Mousekin despite comparable short scale: **no animal ears, muzzle, paws or tails**.
- Gnome inventiveness as an aesthetic theme: mechanical goggles, intricate pouches, clocks/gear motifs, tools, little belts, alchemy gadgets and books. Some references use pointed gnome ears; user may revise their exact ear length or gnome-vs-elf distinctiveness.
- Example skin and culture palettes include fair, peach, tan, olive, brown, deep; this is purely visual variation and not hardwired geography/aptitude.
- Six classic example outfits **Worker, Warrior, Ranger, Mage, Tinkerer, Elder**, plus expressions, hair variants, skin-tone swatches, body types, close-ups, multi-angle short-body turnaround and size comparisons.
- Match `RABBITKIN_STYLE_LOCK.md`: purposeful chunky crisp retro pixel clusters, thick dark outlines, selective dithering, ornate cream parchment and deep gold framing, lavish readable detail.
