## Universal style (all Knomira races)
- Ornate crunchy retro-fantasy **pixel art** with intentional clustered pixels, visible stepped edges, dark charcoal/plum contour lines, selective pixel dithering and crisp highlights.
- Dense but readable outlines and silhouettes; hand-crafted layered fantasy costumes (buckles, belts, leather, pouches, embroidery, hardware) and clear role props.
- Expressive anime-inspired eyes/faces, compact adult fantasy-game proportions, well-separated materials, consistent light direction and native-scale readability.
- Concept-sheet presentation: warm parchment, gold ornamental frame, title/scale sidebar, 3×2 role lineup, labels, separate face/ears/tail/fur/costume breakdowns.
- Do **not** use smooth vector strokes, 3D render/photoreal skin, painterly blur, excessive gradients, generic sparse costumes, or inconsistent pixel scale.
- All race art should share rendering grammar, NOT identical anatomy or accent colors.

## Rabbitkin-specific module
- Adult, gnome-sized, petite/short and stocky/robust; thick thighs, sturdy short legs, large expressive heads, rabbit snouts and blush, oversized upright/swept/lop ears, fluffy compact cottontails.
- Fur/coat variants: pink, lilac, cream, tan/brown; striped, spotted, patched and gradient examples. Exposed anatomy/ears/tails must stay legible.
- Rabbitkin costume accents: cream, teal, charcoal, gold-orange; role-specific apparel in six *concept* examples: Worker, Warrior, Ranger, Mage, Tinkerer, Elder.
- Approximate illustration palette only (not production-indexed): `#F6E5CE #F4A6AA #B88372 #645050 #28222E #2B666C #719A91 #EEA229`.
- The illustration's printed 0.9–1.1m size annotation is art direction, not verified code/collision scale.

## Reusable style prompt
> Ornate, crunchy retro-fantasy pixel art matching the approved Knomira Rabbitkin reference sheet: intentional clustered pixels, visibly stepped outlines, dark charcoal-plum contour lines, high-detail readable silhouettes, selective dithering, warm cream highlights, controlled saturated accents, role-specific miniature equipment, intricate layered fantasy clothing and gold ornaments, expressive anime-inspired faces and compact adult game-sprite proportions. Clean material separation and consistent lighting. Not smooth, vector, airbrushed, photoreal, 3D, blurry or inconsistently pixel-scaled.

## Consistency and safety
The reference is **concept art**, not a production-ready atlas or a validated animation sheet. Compare new output at 1× native game size. Preserve original sprite IDs, source-sheet coordinates and alpha transparency until actual source textures and lookup code have been verified. Never overwrite existing atlas cells or introduce a new playable race/system during the stabilization baseline; defer game integration.
