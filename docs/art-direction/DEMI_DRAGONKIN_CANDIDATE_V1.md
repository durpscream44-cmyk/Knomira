## Must-have mostly human anatomy
- Adult human face, ears, nose, lips, long hair, regular human arms/legs/torso, prominent defined abs and strong but refined musculature, female proportions. Skin generally humanlike rather than covered with all-over dragon scales.
- **Only small localized patches of colored dragon scales on upper thighs/outer hips**, with optional subtle transition into clothing edges. Avoid large all-over body plates.
- **Claws on the hands and feet**: elegant sharp nails or subtle draconic claws rather than full reptilian paw/hindleg anatomy. Keep fingers/toes human-shaped.
- Avoid full dragon muzzle/snouts, huge horns, wings, lengthy draconic tails or heavy reptilian faces; user specifically asked **mostly human save for claws and thigh scales**. The candidate image depicts a clean human-based figure with hands/feet and thigh pattern studies.
- Concept scale annotated c. 1.65–1.90 m; not validated in game.
