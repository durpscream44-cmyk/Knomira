## Anatomical style lock
- Fully anthropomorphic adult **female hyena-folk** with unmistakable animal hyena muzzles, broad toothy jaws and iconic grinning/laughing expression; naturally spotted/striped fur across face and body, round upright ears, shaggy mane/neck ruff, thick expressive hyena tails, padded paw-like hands and feet, claws.
- Strong, muscular and physically imposing warrior silhouettes, with options for **lithe/toned, athletic, muscular, plump/curvy and voluptuous** female builds. Distinct from Wolfkin (wolf muzzle) and Tigerkin (feline muzzle).
- Coat/pattern families: spotted classic, striped/hyena-striped, shaggy brown, pale/light, dark/melanistic, sandy/golden, ruddy/red-tan, gray/slate, faded sand-gray, arcane violet/tan. Illustrative skin and fur variation, not engineered biomes or game abilities.
- Preserve the signature **hyena smile** in the race identity, but retain diverse emotions: Neutral, Happy, Mischievous Smile, Laughing, Smug, Fierce, Surprised, Playful.
- Anatomical studies: muzzle/grin, canine teeth, paw hands/feet, ears, mane/neck fur, varied tail lengths, front/side/back turnarounds. The source labels about **1.65–1.95 m** female concept height; this is not measured engine height.
- Detailed gold/parchment fantasy compendium of six visual roles: **Worker, Warrior, Ranger, Mage, Tinkerer, Elder**. The outfits are examples, not implemented job mechanics.

## Differentiation from approved Demi-Hyenakin
The later approved [Demi-Hyenakin](DEMI_HYENAKIN_BASELINE_V1.md) inherits ears, markings, tails, forearm/thigh fur and smile, but is **about 90% human with a visibly human face and nose** and darker skin palette. Feral Hyenakin is an independently approved full-anthro race; never merge its animal muzzle with the Demi baseline.
