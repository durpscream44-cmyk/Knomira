## Species appearance — distinguish from Dragonkin
- Adult female **anthropomorphic lizardfolk**, upright and humanoid in proportion, with **soft non-threatening lizard-shaped snouts/muzzles**, visible reptilian nostrils, expressive slit-like eyes, small claws and long smooth/scaled lizard tails.
- The user requested **primarily smooth scales**, but with **more pronounced scale plates or mosaic-like patches across outer thighs/hips and shoulders/upper arms**. Balance smooth torso surfaces with those heavier accent scales; do not cover characters uniformly in hard dragon armor plates.
- Attractive mature/athletic and curvy female figures, sturdy reptilian legs and long thick tails. NOT Dragonborn-style long draconic heads, giant horns or mandatory wings. Do not merge with the previously approved [Dragonkin](DRAGONKIN_BASELINE_V1.md) race.
- **Mesoamerican/Aztec-inspired fantasy ornamentation:** bright feathered head crests, gold geometric jewelry, elaborate ceremonial neckpieces and headbands, woven geometric textiles, colorful ceremonial tools, carved sun-serpent motifs and optional large round sun-insignia shields. Respect these as *fantasy design references*; avoid implying an actual historical culture or importing religious claims without further research.
- Sheath/armor and garments are richly layered yet leave pattern regions on arms/hips/thighs readable. Distinctive red/turquoise feathers, jade/teal jewels, cream/burgundy cloth and gold metalwork.
- Source illustration annotates approximate **1.65–1.85 m** adult female height. This is a **concept label**, NOT verified game scale.

## Six illustrated roles
1. **Worker:** farmer, gatherer and artisan, fruit basket and layered gold-trimmed red textiles.
2. **Warrior:** ornate guardian with large patterned shield and stone/metal ceremonial blade.
3. **Ranger:** archer/tracker with bow, quiver and feathered crest.
4. **Mage:** shaman/invoker with turquoise magic and ritual text.
5. **Tinkerer:** engineer/artificer/alchemist with goggles, pouches and bright crystal.
6. **Elder:** richly dressed matriarch/high priestess/regent with sun-serpent staff and ceremonial plumage.

These are *costume/art examples*, not proof of any racial abilities, jobs or magic systems implemented in Knomira.
