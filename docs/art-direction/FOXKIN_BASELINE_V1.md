## Body, cultural and tonal design lock
- Fully anthropomorphic **female foxfolk** (not merely women with pasted fox ears) with charming refined expressions, pointed fox ears, recognizable fluffy fox cheek/muzzle fur, paw-hands and paws/ankles, expressive bushy foxtails.
- Human-adjacent female height (the concept sheet says **about 1.50–1.70 m**); sleek, agile build with adult feminine curves. Foxlike feral confidence with cunning, flirtatious/sly, playful, mischievous expressions and a courtly **ojou-sama** elegance. Character traits are artistic direction, not hard-coded racial morality.
- East-Asian-inspired *fantasy* visual language: floral hair ornaments, patterned kimono-like layers, fans, bows, festival tassels, decorative cords, gold filigree and rich reds/plums. Inspiration rather than depiction of one real culture.
- Maintain readable muzzle and facial markings: red fox, silver fox, arctic fox, fennec, cross fox, crimson fox, kitsune multi-tail, shadow fox. Tails vary standard/fluffy/three/five/seven-tailed/sleek and preserve species-correct volume.
- Recognizable fur patterns should carry through facial cheeks, wrists, thighs, shins and tails. Foxkin are visually distinct from **Felikin** cats and **Wolfkin** canines, and from mostly human Canidemi.
- Concept sheet shows six role costumes **Worker, Warrior, Ranger, Mage, Tinkerer, Elder** plus facial expression, ear, tail, paw and body shape variants, fur-pattern examples, height silhouettes and front/side/back turnarounds.
- Palette: gold, vermilion, rust-orange, cream, charcoal, crimson, purple and forest green. **Universal visual grammar**: ornate crunchy retro-fantasy pixel art, deliberate stepped clusters, strong charcoal-plum contour lines, selective dithering, high-detail tiny equipment and gold-framed parchment panels (see `RABBITKIN_STYLE_LOCK.md`).
