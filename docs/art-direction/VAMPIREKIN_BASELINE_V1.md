## Canonical appearance
- **Adult female vampires**, primarily human facial and body anatomy with subtly visible pointed canine teeth (fangs), striking crimson/wine, amber or violet eyes, refined aristocratic beauty and poised gothic countess silhouettes.
- Characteristic **black fitted corsets and bodices**, ornate lace, layered dark/burgundy skirts or capes, fitted dark thigh-high legwear, dark gloves, fine chains, gold accents, rose-themed ornaments and dramatic gothic luxury. Variation from worker/steward attire through armor, hooded ranger outfit, spellcaster robes and elaborate elder matriarch regalia.
- Human-proportioned hands/feet, regular humanlike faces, graceful adult feminine curves and varied hairstyles. **Do not** automatically add animal muzzles, demon horns, wings, tails or all-over fur; those belong to other Knomira species.
- Hair examples in source: Long/Wavy, Half-Up/Flowing, Updo/Ornate, Braided/Side, High Pony/Ribboned. Color varies naturally, including pale silver/ash and dark black. Skin-tone examples **Pale Ivory (Aristocratic), Porcelain (Cool), Dusk Rose (Warm), Olive Dusk (Rich)**; these are visual choices **without gameplay or personality implication**.
- Eye examples: Crimson (Classic), Wine Red (Intense), Amber (Predatory), Violet (Arcane). Canines/fangs: Subtle, Clear, Pronounced, Elegant. Cape options include Fur-Lined Mantle, embroidered noble cape, short practical cape, shoulder drape and ceremonial veiled cape.
- Original sheet shows a **concept adult height ~1.60–1.78 m**. It is a drawing annotation, not a validated engine unit or collider specification.
